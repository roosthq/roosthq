# Learning media credits

Every photograph, diagram and animal sound used in Roost HQ's Learning questions is a real, freely licensed file from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/). Images were cropped or fitted to 4:3 and converted to WebP; sounds were trimmed, loudness-normalized, and converted to MP3. Each work stays under its original license (linked below). Nothing here is synthesized. The math and logic pictures (clocks, fractions, graphs, patterns) are original drawings generated by the app's own tooling.
